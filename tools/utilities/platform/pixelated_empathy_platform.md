## 🎯 **Six Challenging Client Personalities for Training**

### **Difficult Client Types Available:**

1. **🛡️ Resistant Client**
   - **Traits:** Defensive, mistrustful, intellectualizing, minimizing
   - **Challenges:** Building rapport with unmotivated client
   - **Learning Goals:** Motivational interviewing, resistance management

2. **🔥 Hostile/Aggressive Client**
   - **Traits:** Angry, blaming, intimidating, volatile
   - **Challenges:** De-escalation, boundary setting, staying therapeutic
   - **Learning Goals:** Crisis de-escalation, anger management techniques

3. **💔 Borderline Traits Client**
   - **Traits:** Emotional instability, fear of abandonment, splitting
   - **Challenges:** Managing emotional flooding, consistent boundaries
   - **Learning Goals:** DBT skills, therapeutic consistency, crisis management

4. **👑 Narcissistic Traits Client**
   - **Traits:** Grandiose, entitled, lacking empathy, exploitative
   - **Challenges:** Addressing grandiosity, finding underlying vulnerability
   - **Learning Goals:** Therapeutic confrontation, empathy building

5. **🆘 Suicidal Ideation Client**
   - **Traits:** Hopeless, desperate, ambivalent, crisis-focused
   - **Challenges:** Suicide risk assessment, safety planning, hope building
   - **Learning Goals:** Crisis intervention, safety assessment, suicide
     prevention

6. **😰 Trauma-Reactive Client**
   - **Traits:** Hypervigilant, dissociative, easily triggered, avoidant
   - **Challenges:** Creating safety, managing triggers, trauma processing
   - **Learning Goals:** Trauma-informed care, grounding techniques, safety
     creation
