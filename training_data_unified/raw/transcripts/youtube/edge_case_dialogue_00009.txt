The dimly lit room was thick with tension, the air heavy with the scent of stale coffee and worn leather. The client's chair was positioned in a way that made it seem like they were trapped, with the therapist's imposing figure looming across from them. The client, a disheveled young woman with dark circles under her eyes, fidgeted with the hem of her jeans, her eyes darting around the room like a trapped animal.

**Therapist:** (leaning back in her chair, steepling her fingers) So, you've been having some... issues. (her voice was detached, with a hint of disdain) Tell me, what's been going on?

**Client:** (nervously) I don't know, it's just... everything feels like it's falling apart. (her voice cracked, and she looked down, her hands shaking)

**Therapist:** (skeptical) Oh, everything? That's quite a broad statement. Can you be more specific? (she raised an eyebrow, her tone dripping with incredulity)

**Client:** (hesitant, glancing around the room) I... I've been having nightmares. And I just feel so anxious all the time. (her voice was barely above a whisper)

**Therapist:** (dismissively) Nightmares, huh? That's quite common. I'm sure it's just stress. (she made a note on her pad, her hand moving with a swift, almost dismissive motion)

**Client:** (anger rising) You don't understand. It's not just stress. It's like... (she paused, her eyes welling up with tears) it's like I'm reliving it all over again.

The therapist's expression remained unimpressed. _Dramatic much?_

**Therapist:** (leaning forward, her voice taking on a slightly condescending tone) Oh, I'm sure it's very traumatic for you. But let's not get ahead of ourselves. Can you tell me more about these nightmares? (her eyes locked onto the client's, her gaze intense)

**Client:** (her voice shaking, tears streaming down her face) They're about... about what happened. The abuse. The pain. (she choked on the last word, her body beginning to shake)

The therapist's internal monologue: _Okay, now we're getting somewhere. Or not._

**Therapist:** (pushing further, her tone slightly antagonistic) And how do you think this relates to your current situation? Are you sure you're not just... (she paused, her expression suggesting she was searching for the right word) ...milking it for sympathy?

**Client:** (exploding) You're not listening! You don't care! You're just like all the others – you just want to hurt me more! (she stood up, her chair scraping against the floor, and took a step towards the therapist)

**Therapist:** (taken aback, trying to regain control) Okay, let's take a step back. (she held up her hands, a placating gesture) I apologize if I came across as insensitive.

**Client:** (seething, her voice low and menacing) You have no idea what it's like. You're just a... a... (she spat out the word) vulture, preying on people's pain.

The therapist's internal monologue: _I've gone too far. I'm losing control of this session._

**Therapist:** (trying to salvage the situation) I understand that you're upset. Let's try to... (she reached out, attempting to touch the client's arm)

**Client:** (jerking away, her eyes blazing) Don't touch me! (she backed away, her voice dropping to a whisper) I'm going to report you. You're not fit to be a therapist.

The therapist's anxiety spiked, her mind racing with the potential consequences. _I've blown it. This is going to end badly._

**Therapist:** (desperate to regain control) Wait, let's not be hasty. (she stood up, her eyes locked on the client's)

**Client:** (her voice dripping with venom) You're the one who's being hasty. You're the one who's going to pay. (she turned and stormed out of the room, slamming the door behind her)
