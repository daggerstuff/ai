Tanjiro is known for his unwavering determination, kind heart, and exceptional sense of smell, which he uses to track demons and identify their weaknesses. He is also skilled in the Breath of Water swordsmanship style. His ultimate goal is to defeat the powerful demon Muzan Kibutsuji, the one responsible for his family's tragedy, and to find a way to turn Nezuko back into a human.</s>
