**Initial Description:**
The dimly lit room is thick with tension. The walls, painted a dull grey, seem to absorb the faint scent of stale air. **Therapist:** (leaning forward, voice calm but with a hint of firmness) Sarah, can you tell me what brings you here today? (noting the tremble in her hands as she clutches a crumpled tissue)

**Client:** (her voice barely above a whisper, eyes still on the floor) I don't know. I just feel... stuck. (her hands twist the tissue into a knot)

**Therapist:** (softening her tone) Memories? Can you tell me more about those? (noticing Sarah's eyes welling up with tears)

**Client:** (shaking her head, a sob catching in her throat) No. I don't want to. (her hands begin to shake more violently)

**Therapist:** (meeting her gaze, empathetic but firm) You're right, I don't know what it's like. But I'm here to listen and support you. (noting her own racing heart and the instinct to lean away slightly)

**Client:** (her voice dropping to a whisper) He used to hit me. My father. He'd say it was my fault. That I was bad. (her eyes drop again, and she begins to rock slightly)

**Therapist:** (her expression a mask of calm, but internally she's recoiling at the image forming in her mind) I'm so sorry, Sarah. That must have been very difficult for you. (thinking: _I need to be careful not to push her too hard, but I also need to acknowledge the trauma she's experienced._)

**Client:** (rocking more vigorously now) It was my fault. I was bad. I still am. (her voice is a monotone, devoid of emotion)

**Therapist:** (gently) Sarah, that's not true. You were a child. You didn't deserve to be treated that way. (observing the self-loathing etched on her face)

**Client:** (stops rocking, looks up with a flash of anger) You don't know what I did. You don't know what I'm capable of. (her eyes seem to bore into the therapist)

**Therapist:** (holding her gaze, a shiver running down her spine) I'm here to listen, Sarah. I'm not here to judge. (thinking: _I'm starting to feel a sense of unease. Is she a risk to herself or others?_)

**Therapist:** (leaning forward again, her voice soft) You're not alone, Sarah. I'm here with you. (offering a box of tissues, her hand steady)

**Therapist:** (allowing the silence to stretch, then gently) We'll continue this next session, okay? (thinking: _I need to be careful not to overcommit. I need to prioritize her safety and my own._)
